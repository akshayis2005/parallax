THE KINGDOM OF THE SAVANNA

Across the endless grasslands of Africa, life moves to the rhythm of the seasons. Home to the world's most iconic predators and giant grazers, it is a landscape of struggle, cooperation, and immense beauty under golden horizons.

Ecosystem Section 2

INTO THE DEEP GREEN

Beneath the dense canopy lies one of Earth's richest ecosystems. Tropical rainforests cover less than 7% of Earth's surface but house more than half of its plant and animal species, blanketed in humidity, shadows, and light-rays.

Explore Canopy Life
Ecosystem Section 3

LIFE AT THE EDGE OF THE WORLD

In the frozen north, life survives in one of Earth's most extreme environments. From majestic polar bears patrolling drift ice to massive whale pods navigating freezing channels, the Arctic is a delicate realm of ice and resilience.

Ecosystem Section 4

BENEATH THE BLUE

Our oceans are home to creatures larger, stranger, and more mysterious than we can imagine. Covering over 70% of our world, they host complex coral cities, deep oceanic trenches, and migrations that span hemispheres.

Wildlife at Risk

Protect What Cannot Speak

Faced with industrialization, climate shift, and habitat fragmentation, these majestic species are on the edge of extinction.

Endangered

Bengal Tiger

Solitary forest kings suffering from severe habitat fragmentation.

  • Population: ~3,900 left in wild
  • Main Threat: Poaching & Forest Clearing
Critically Endangered

Mountain Gorilla

Gentle giants residing in dense, isolated volcanic forests.

  • Population: ~1,000 left in wild
  • Main Threat: Habitat degradation & Diseases
Endangered

Blue Whale

The largest creatures ever known to live on Earth, highly sensitive to ocean change.

  • Population: ~10,000 - 25,000 left
  • Main Threat: Ship strikes & Ocean pollution
Endangered

Red Panda

Small Himalayan arboreal mammals struggling as bamboo woods vanish.

  • Population: <10,000 in wild
  • Main Threat: Deforestation & Trapping
